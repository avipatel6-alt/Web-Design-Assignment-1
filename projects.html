My Projects

Here are some projects and assignments I completed in previous semesters.

Portfolio Website

I built this website from scratch using HTML5 and CSS3, applying what I learned in my course to create a functional, polished and consistent design across four pages. The project covered semantic page structure, a shared header and navigation on every page, a responsive grid layout for the cards and page sections, and media embedded with images and video. I focused on keeping the styling in a single stylesheet so the whole site stays consistent and is easy to update

Crafting a trading indicator

I developed a custom trading indicator for my own trading portfolio, built on technical analysis principles. It is designed to identify potential buy and sell signals in the stock market using defined, rules-based conditions rather than gut feeling. I tested the logic against historical price data to see how it behaved before relying on it, and I keep refining it as part of my broader goal of a disciplined, data-driven approach to risk and execution

Advanced Networking typology

My group members and I built a comprehensive model of an advanced network for a fictional company. The project focused on integrating multiple protocols and technologies to improve network performance and security, and it included planning the physical cabling and layout. Working as a team meant dividing the design work, documenting our decisions, and checking that each part worked together as one network

Enterprise management

My group members and I designed and implemented an enterprise management system for a fictional company. The goal was to streamline business processes and improve operational efficiency by bringing day-to-day workflows into one organized system. We worked through requirements, design and implementation together, and the project gave me experience collaborating on a larger build with shared responsibilities
